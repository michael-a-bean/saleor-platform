## Archive Decision (2026-01-28)

Per Council debate, plans were archived because:

1. **Plans had become tracking artifacts** - not active work guides
2. **Completion percentages were misleading** - "85% complete" doesn't mean 15% effort remaining
3. **Organic work happened outside plans** - offline mode commits weren't documented
4. **Plan completion is not the goal** - shipping working software is

Going forward:
- Use focused issues for remaining work
- No new comprehensive plans without explicit scope
- Work competes for prioritization on its own merit
